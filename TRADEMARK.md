## What you may do without asking

- Use, study, change, fork and redistribute the code under the Apache License.
- Say that your work is **based on**, **a fork of**, or **compatible with** Hivemind Shape
  Studio. Truthful, descriptive references are always fine.
- Keep the `NOTICE` file and the author lines in the source, which the licence requires.
- Write articles, tutorials and reviews using the names to refer to this project.

## What needs permission

- Shipping a fork, build, service or product **under the marks** above, or under a name or
  logo confusingly similar to them. A fork must get its own name.
- Presenting a build as **official**, **verified** or **endorsed** by Hivemind. Official
  builds are only those published through the channels in `NOTICE` (GitHub Releases of this
  repository and the `hivemind-shapestudio` package on PyPI).
- Using the marks in a company name, domain name, app-store listing or social handle.
- Selling merchandise with the marks.

Ask by opening an issue on the repository or contacting the author through GitHub. Permission
for community projects is normally given quickly.

## The verified catalogue

The models in `registry.py` carry a `verified` field that says exactly what the Hivemind
project has done with them: pinned by hash, licence checked, run end to end on which
hardware. A fork may keep the list but may not describe models it adds as "Hivemind verified".

## Attribution line

Official builds carry the line **"Shape Studio by Hivemind · made with ♥ by Ratul Roy
(royal-programmer)"** in the app and on the website. Forks must not carry it unchanged; they
should state their own maintainer and may add "based on Hivemind Shape Studio".
